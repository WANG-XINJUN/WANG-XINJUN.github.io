About Me

I am an Associate Professor at Shandong Normal University. My research interests focus on nonlinear control systems, multi-agent systems, cyber-physical systems security, adaptive control, and resilient control against cyber attacks.

Research Interests

Nonlinear Control Systems

Analysis and control of nonlinear dynamical systems.

Multi-Agent Systems

Consensus, cooperative control and distributed control.

Secure Control

Resilient control under cyber attacks and communication constraints.

Adaptive Control

Adaptive and event-triggered control for networked systems.
