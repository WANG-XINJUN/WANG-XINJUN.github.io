Research Interests

Nonlinear Systems

Adaptive control, tracking control, stabilization and finite-time control of nonlinear systems.

Multi-Agent Systems

Consensus tracking, distributed control, bipartite consensus and cooperative control.

Cyber Security

Deception attacks, DoS attacks, cyber-physical systems and resilient control.

Adaptive Control

Adaptive control, fuzzy control, output-feedback control and unknown system dynamics.

Event-Triggered Control

Event-triggered mechanisms, intermittent communication and resource-efficient networked control.

Fault-Tolerant Control

Actuator faults, fault detection, fault reconstruction and resilient fault-tolerant control.
