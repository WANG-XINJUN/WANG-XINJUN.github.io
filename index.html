About Me

I am an Associate Professor at Shandong Normal University. My research focuses on nonlinear systems, multi-agent systems, cyber-physical systems, adaptive control and secure control.

My research interests include nonlinear control, distributed coordination, consensus tracking, and resilient control against cyber attacks.
