Academic Profile

Xinjun Wang is an Associate Professor at Shandong Normal University. His research interests include nonlinear systems, multi-agent systems, cyber attack and adaptive control.

His research focuses particularly on secure and resilient control of networked nonlinear systems under deception attacks, denial-of-service attacks, intermittent communication and actuator faults.
