About Me

I am an Associate Professor at Shandong Normal University. My research focuses on nonlinear control systems, multi-agent systems, cyber-physical systems, adaptive control and resilient control under cyber attacks.

My research interests include secure control of nonlinear systems, distributed control, consensus tracking, deception attacks, denial-of-service attacks and fault-tolerant control.

Research Interests

Nonlinear Control

Adaptive control, tracking control, stabilization, finite-time control and prescribed-performance control.

Multi-Agent Systems

Distributed control, consensus tracking, bipartite consensus and networked coordination.

Secure Control

Resilient control under deception attacks, DoS attacks, sensor attacks and actuator faults.
